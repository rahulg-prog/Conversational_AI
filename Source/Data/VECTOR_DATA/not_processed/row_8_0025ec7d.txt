The Honda Dio 110 is a scooter designed to deliver efficient urban mobility. It features a 4-stroke, SI engine with a displacement of 109.51cc, compliant with OBD2B standards, generating a maximum power output of 5.85 kW at 8000 rpm. The fuel system includes a 5.3-liter fuel tank, supporting an impressive mileage of 50 km per liter as reported online. This model is equipped with front and rear tyres sized at 90/90-12 54J and 90/100-10 53J respectively, ensuring stable and controlled handling. For braking, it utilizes 130mm drum brakes on both the front and rear wheels. Suspension duties are managed by a telescopic front fork and a unit swing rear setup, contributing to a comfortable ride. Honda has incorporated advanced technology such as an LED headlamp for enhanced visibility, powered by a 12V 5Ah battery. Safety and convenience are addressed through features including a TFT meter, idling stop system, USB-C charging port, ACG silent start, and PGM-FI fuel injection. Core functionalities are further supported by a side stand engine cut-off, combined braking system (CBS), and the prominent LED headlamp. The scooter is available in a variety of colors, including Imperial Red Metallic, Mat Marvel Blue, Mat Axis Gray Metallic, Pearl Igneous Black, and Pearl Deep Ground Gray. The seat height is not specified for this model.
