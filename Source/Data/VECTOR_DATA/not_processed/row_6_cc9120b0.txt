The Honda Unicorn is a well-engineered bike categorized under two-wheelers, featuring a 4-stroke, spark-ignition engine with a displacement of 162.7cc that complies with OBD2B standards. It delivers a maximum power output of 9.5 kW at 7,500 rpm, ensuring a balance of performance and efficiency. Equipped with a 13-liter fuel tank, the Honda Unicorn offers an impressive mileage of 60 km per liter, as reported by internet sources. The bike’s wheel system includes front tyres sized 80/100-18 and rear tyres 100/90-18, providing stability and grip. For braking, it incorporates a front disc brake measuring 240mm and a rear drum brake of 130mm, enhanced by a single-channel ABS system, which contributes to rider safety alongside an engine cut-off feature. Suspension responsibilities are managed by telescopic forks at the front and a monoshock setup at the rear, ensuring a smooth and controlled ride experience. Technologically, the Honda Unicorn integrates PGM-FI fuel injection and enhanced Smart Power (eSP) technology, which collectively improve fuel efficiency and engine responsiveness. Its core features include a long seat designed for rider comfort, chrome styling for an appealing aesthetic, and a reputation as a reliable commuter bike. Available in three distinctive colors—Pearl Igneous Black, Imperial Red Metallic, and Matte Axis Gray Metallic—the Honda Unicorn has a seat height of 798mm, providing an accessible riding position for a wide range of riders.
