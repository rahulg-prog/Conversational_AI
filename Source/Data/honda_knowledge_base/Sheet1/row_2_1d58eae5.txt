The Honda Shine 100 is a bike equipped with a 4-stroke, SI engine of 100cc capacity, compliant with OBD2B standards. Delivering a maximum power output of 7.38 PS at 7,500 rpm, it offers efficient performance alongside a fuel tank capacity of 9 liters. The mileage is rated at an impressive 55 kmpl based on internet sources. This model features alloy wheels and incorporates the Combi-Brake System (CBS) with an equalizer for enhanced braking safety. Its suspension system includes perfect suspension dynamics with telescopic hydraulic components, contributing to a balanced ride experience. Advanced technologies integrated into the Honda Shine 100 include eSP (Enhanced Smart Power), PGM-FI (Programmed Fuel Injection), and CBS. Safety is further reinforced with CBS and a side-stand engine cut-off mechanism. The bike is designed with core features such as stylish graphics, a long seat, and a lightweight frame, improving both aesthetics and comfort. Available color options include Blue with Black, Gray with Black, Orange with Black, Red with Black, and Green with Black. The seat height is set at 786mm, providing a comfortable riding posture.
