The Honda NX200 is a bike equipped with a 4-stroke, SI engine that displaces 184.4cc and complies with OBD2B emissions standards. It delivers a maximum power output of 12.5 kW at 8,500 rpm, providing a balanced blend of performance and efficiency. The fuel capacity is 12 liters, supporting a mileage of approximately 35 km per liter as reported online. This model features front and rear tires sized 110/70-17 M/C 54S and 140/70-17 M/C 66S, respectively, offering stability and grip suitable for a variety of riding conditions.

Braking is managed by front and rear disc brakes measuring 276mm and 220mm, respectively, enhanced by dual channel ABS which ensures superior safety and control. The suspension setup includes an upside-down (USD) fork at the front and a monoshock at the rear, designed to deliver a smooth and responsive ride. Technological integrations aboard the Honda NX200 include Honda RoadSync connectivity, Honda Selectable Torque Control (HSTC), an assist slipper clutch for better downshifting, and the PGM-FI fuel injection system.

Safety is further enhanced with the inclusion of dual channel ABS, HSTC, and a hazard switch, reinforcing the bike’s rider protection features. Core attributes extend to a TFT display that presents essential riding information clearly, Bluetooth connectivity for seamless device pairing, built-in navigation, and USB-C charging for on-the-go power needs. Available in three distinctive colors—Athletic Blue Metallic, Radiant Red Metallic, and Pearl Igneous Black—the Honda NX200 also offers a seat height of 810 mm, striking a balance between comfort and sportiness suitable for diverse rider heights.
