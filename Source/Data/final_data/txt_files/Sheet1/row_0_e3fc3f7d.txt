The Honda Livo is a bike equipped with a 4 Stroke, SI Engine that has a displacement of 109.51cc and complies with OBD2B standards. It delivers a maximum power output of 6.47 kW at 7500 rpm, ensuring efficient performance. The vehicle features a 9-liter fuel tank and offers an impressive mileage of 70 km per liter, as reported on the internet. Its tires include an 80/100-18 M/C 47P at the front and an 80/100-18 M/C 54P at the rear, providing balanced grip and stability. For braking, the Honda Livo is equipped with a front disc brake measuring 240mm or a 130mm drum brake, along with a 130mm rear drum brake that incorporates a Combined Braking System (CBS) for enhanced safety. The suspension system comprises telescopic forks at the front and a hydraulic-type setup at the rear, contributing to a comfortable and controlled ride. Technological features integrated into the bike include enhanced Smart Power (eSP), Programmed Fuel Injection (PGM-FI), an ACG starter, and the CBS with equalizer for optimized braking response. Safety is further supported by a side stand engine cut-off mechanism. The Honda Livo boasts a stylish design complemented by a digital meter and alloy wheels, emphasizing both aesthetics and functionality. Available in colors such as Athletic Blue Metallic, Matte Axis Gray, Imperial Red Metallic, and Black, this model offers versatile visual appeal. The seat height is set at 790mm, providing an ergonomic riding position suitable for a wide range of riders.
