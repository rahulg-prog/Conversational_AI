The Honda SP160 is a sporty bike equipped with a 4-stroke, SI engine that boasts a displacement of 162.71cc and complies with OBD2B standards. Delivering a maximum power output of 9.9 kW at 7,500 rpm, it features a 12-liter fuel tank and offers an impressive mileage of 50 km per liter, as reported online. The bike is supported by front and rear tyres sized 80/100-17 and 130/70-17, respectively, ensuring stability and grip. Braking is managed through a 276mm front disc, with the rear brake available as either a disc or drum, complemented by a dual-channel ABS system on selected variants for enhanced safety. Suspension is handled by telescopic forks at the front and a monoshock setup at the rear, contributing to a comfortable and controlled ride. Advanced technology integrations include Honda’s eSP and PGM-FI systems, a digital console, USB-C charging port, and ABS functionality. For safety, the bike comes equipped with ABS and an engine cut-off feature. Its core design elements highlight a muscular fuel tank and an advanced digital meter, emphasizing a sporty aesthetic. The seat height stands at 796mm, catering to rider ergonomics, while the bike is available in a range of colors, including red, black, blue, and gray metallic.
