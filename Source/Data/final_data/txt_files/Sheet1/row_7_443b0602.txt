The Honda Activa 125 is a scooter known for its efficient performance and advanced features. It is powered by a 4-stroke, SI engine with a displacement of 123.92cc, compliant with OBD2B standards, delivering a maximum power output of 6.20 kW at 6500 rpm. The vehicle is equipped with a 5.3-liter fuel tank, offering an impressive mileage of 47 kmpl as per internet sources. For road handling and comfort, the Activa 125 features front tires sized 90/90-12 54J and rear tires sized 90/100-10 53J. Its braking system includes a 190mm front disc brake and a 130mm rear drum brake, ensuring reliable stopping power. Suspension is managed through a telescopic front setup alongside a 3-step adjustable spring-loaded hydraulic rear suspension, enhancing ride comfort and stability. The scooter incorporates modern technology such as an LED headlamp, a DC electrical system supported by a 12V 5Ah battery, and advanced safety elements including a smart key, TFT meter, Honda RoadSync connectivity, idling stop system, PGM-FI fuel injection, an ACG starter, and a USB-C port. Core usability features highlight a side stand engine cut-off, telescopic suspension, a combined braking system with disc brake options, and a conveniently placed glove box. The Honda Activa 125 is available in a variety of colors including Rebel Red Metallic, Pearl Igneous Black, Mat Axis Gray Metallic, Pearl Deep Ground Gray, Pearl Precious White, and Pearl Siren Blue, with a seat height of 165 mm that caters to a comfortable riding posture.
