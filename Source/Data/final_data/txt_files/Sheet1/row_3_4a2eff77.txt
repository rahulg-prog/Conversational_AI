The Honda Shine 125 is a bike equipped with a 4-stroke, SI engine boasting a displacement of 123.94cc, compliant with OBD2B standards. It delivers a maximum power output of 7.93 kW at 7500 rpm, supported by a fuel tank capacity of 10.5 liters. Notably efficient, the vehicle offers a mileage of 65 km per liter as reported online. The tyres are specified as 80/100-18 M/C 47P at the front and 90/90-18 M/C 51P at the rear, providing stability and grip. For braking, it features a front disc brake of 240mm paired with a 130mm drum brake at the rear, supported by a Combined Braking System (CBS) for enhanced safety. The suspension setup includes a telescopic front fork and a hydraulic-type rear suspension, ensuring a comfortable ride. Technological aspects of the Honda Shine 125 include enhanced Smart Power (eSP), Programmed Fuel Injection (PGM-FI), a fully digital meter, idling stop system, ACG starter, and a USB-C port for connectivity. Safety measures are reinforced with CBS and an engine cut-off feature activated by the side stand. Core features also highlight the fully digital meter, a wider rear tyre, and chrome styling for an appealing look. This model is available in a selection of colors such as Decent Blue Metallic, Pearl Siren Blue, Geny Gray Metallic, Rebel Red Metallic, Pearl Igneous Black, and Matte Axis Gray Metallic. The seat height stands at 719mm, balancing accessibility and rider comfort.
