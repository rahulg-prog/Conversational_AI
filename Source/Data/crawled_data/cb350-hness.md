## Frequently Asked Questions

##### 1. What braking system is used in the  Honda CB350 H'ness?

Arrow

<!-- image -->

The motorcycle has a dual-channel ABS (Anti-lock Braking System) with a 310 mm front disc brake and a 240 mm rear disc brake, which ensures excellent braking performance and safety.

##### 2. What type of suspension does the Honda CB350 H'ness feature?

Arrow

<!-- image -->

The **Honda CB350 H'ness** features telescopic forks in the front and twin hydraulic shock absorbers in the rear, providing outstanding riding comfort and stability in various road situations.

##### 3. What colour options are available for the Honda CB350 H'ness?

Arrow

<!-- image -->

The **Honda CB350 H'ness** motorcycles are available in various unique colour schemes that combine contemporary and legacy. Check with your local Honda BigWing dealership for the availability of certain colours.

##### 4. How does the Honda CB350 H'ness?

Arrow

<!-- image -->

The Honda H'ness CB350 Legacy edition's safety features include a dual-channel ABS for controlled braking, Honda Selectable Torque Control (HSTC) to avoid rear-wheel slippage, and an Emergency Stop Signal (ESS) to improve visibility during unexpected stopping.

##### 5. Is the Honda CB350 H'ness compliant with the latest emission norms?

Arrow

<!-- image -->

Yes, the Honda H'ness CB350 complies with the 2025-ready OBD-2B standards.

##### 6. What connectivity features are available in the Honda CB350 H'ness?

Arrow

<!-- image -->

The Honda CB350 H'ness features the Honda RoadSync, allowing riders to connect their smartphones via a specialised app and Bluetooth for navigation, audio management, incoming call notifications, and weather updates.
