## Frequently Asked Questions

##### 1. What makes the CB350RS different from other motorcycles?

Arrow

<!-- image -->

Its scrambler-inspired design and modern features like Honda RoadSync set it apart.

##### 2. Is the CB350RS suitable for long rides?

Arrow

<!-- image -->

Yes, the CB350RS motorcycle's comfortable seat, upright riding position, and fuel-efficient engine make it great for long rides.

##### 3. Does the Honda CB350RS have a good road grip for rough terrains?

Arrow

<!-- image -->

Absolutely! The wider tires and dual-channel ABS provide excellent grip and stability.

##### 4. Can I connect my phone to the CB350RS motorcycle?

Arrow

<!-- image -->

Yes, the semi-digital console supports Bluetooth and voice commands for calls and notifications.

##### 5. Is the Honda CB350RS motorcycle beginner-friendly?

Arrow

<!-- image -->

Definitely! Its smooth handling, upright posture, and digital analogue meter make it ideal for new riders.

##### 6. What's the fuel tank capacity?

Arrow

<!-- image -->

The CB350RS has a 15-litre tank, perfect for long-distance rides without frequent refuelling.
