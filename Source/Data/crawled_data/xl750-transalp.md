## Frequently Asked Questions

##### 1. What kind of engine does the Honda XL750 Transalp have?

Arrow

<!-- image -->

The Honda XL750 Transalp comes with a 755 cc parallel-twin engine producing 67.5 kW and 75 Nm torque.

##### 2. Does the XL750 Transalp support smartphone connectivity?

Arrow

<!-- image -->

Yes, the XL750 Transalp includes Honda RoadSync for navigation, ride data, and call/music functions.

##### 3. Can I ride the Honda XL750 Transalp off-road?

Arrow

<!-- image -->

Absolutely! It features 5 riding modes, switchable rear ABS, and upgraded suspension for off-road adventures.

##### 4. What is the seat height of the XL750 Transalp?

Arrow

<!-- image -->

The XL750 Transalp has a balanced 850 mm seat height, ideal for adventure and touring riders.

##### 5. What's the fuel tank capacity of the XL750 Transalp?

Arrow

<!-- image -->

It boasts a 16.9 L tank, offering extended range for long-distance touring and exploration.
