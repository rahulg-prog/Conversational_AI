## Frequently Asked Questions

##### 1. What is the standard warranty period?

Arrow

<!-- image -->

Honda Dio125 comes with a standard warranty of 3 years, 36000km (whichever is earlier) from date of purchase.

##### 2. What does standard warranty covers?

Arrow

<!-- image -->

Standard warranty usually cover major components and system of the product This  also include repairs of the defect in component or workmanship.

##### 3. What are common exclusion in warranty?

Arrow

<!-- image -->

HMSI does not warrant normal wear and

tear items like Clutch Weight, Clutch Disc,

Brake shoe, Brake Pads, Brake Disc, Drive

belt, Drive Chain, Drive Chain Sprocket,

Wheel Rim (in case of misalignment and

bent), Bushes, Fasteners, Shims, Washers

and Electrical items like Bulbs, Fuses,

Rubber and Plastic Components like

Grommets, O-Rings, Bellows, Packings,

Gaskets, Oil Seals and Consumables like

Fuel Filter, Air Cleaner Element, Engine Oil,

Grease, Suspension Oil, and other items as

specified by HMSI.

##### 4. What is an Extended Warranty?

Arrow

<!-- image -->

An extended warranty is additional warranty coverage offered to customers in addition to the Standard Warranty. It can be purchased from the HMSI Authorized Dealers. You can opt Extend Warranty from 1 year up to 7 years - Total making of 10 years of warranty (3 + 7 years).

##### 5. What is the free service schedule for the Honda Dio125?

Arrow

<!-- image -->

- 1st Free Service: 750-1000 km or 15-30 days
- 2nd Free Service: 5500-6000 km or 165-180 day
- 3rd Free Service: 11500-12000 km or 350-365 day

Days or Kilometers whichever comes earlier from the date of Sale

##### 6. How much storage space does the Dio125 have?

Arrow

<!-- image -->

Do not worry - the Dio125 has plenty of space under the seat for your helmet and daily essentials, making every ride super convenient!

##### 7. What warranty does the Honda Dio125 come with?

Arrow

<!-- image -->

The Dio125 comes with a 3-year warranty, which you can extend up to 7 years.

##### 8. How does the TFT meter enhance the experience?

Arrow

<!-- image -->

The fully TFT meter displays essential information, such as fuel efficiency, trip meters, and service reminders, ensuring a smart and modern riding experience.

##### 9. Can the Honda Dio125 handle rough roads?

Arrow

<!-- image -->

Yes, with its strong suspension, it handles both smooth and bumpy roads comfortably.

##### 10. What fuel system does Dio125 provide?

Arrow

<!-- image -->

The Honda Dio125 allows you to refuel without stepping down from the vehicle.

##### 11. Is the Honda Dio125 suitable for beginners?

Arrow

<!-- image -->

Absolutely! Dio125 is lightweight, and its user-friendly features make it perfect for riders.

##### 12. What is the paid service schedule for Dio125?

Arrow

Every 6000 Kms or Within 6 months
