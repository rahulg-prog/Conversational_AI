## Frequently Asked Questions

##### 1. What is the standard warranty period?

Arrow

<!-- image -->

Honda Activa110 comes with a standard warranty of 3 years, 36000km (whichever is earlier) from date of purchase.

##### 2. What does standard warranty covers?

Arrow

<!-- image -->

Standard warranty usually cover major components and system of the product This  also include repairs of the defect in component or workmanship.

##### 3. What are common exclusion in warranty?

Arrow

<!-- image -->

HMSI does not warrant normal wear and

tear items like Clutch Weight, Clutch Disc,

Brake shoe, Brake Pads, Brake Disc, Drive

belt, Drive Chain, Drive Chain Sprocket,

Wheel Rim (in case of misalignment and

bent), Bushes, Fasteners, Shims, Washers

and Electrical items like Bulbs, Fuses,

Rubber and Plastic Components like

Grommets, O-Rings, Bellows, Packings,

Gaskets, Oil Seals and Consumables like

Fuel Filter, Air Cleaner Element, Engine Oil,

Grease, Suspension Oil, and other items as

specified by HMSI.

##### 4. What is an Extended Warranty?

Arrow

<!-- image -->

An extended warranty is additional warranty coverage offered to customers in addition to the Standard Warranty. It can be purchased from the HMSI Authorized Dealers. You can opt Extend Warranty from 1 year up to 7 years - Total making of 10 years of warranty (3 + 7 years).

##### 5. What is the free service schedule for the Honda Activa110?

Arrow

<!-- image -->

- 1st Free Service: 750-1000 km or 15-30 days
- 2nd Free Service: 5500-6000 km or 165-180 day
- 3rd Free Service: 11500-12000 km or 350-365 day

Days or Kilometers whichever comes earlier from the date of Sale

##### 6. What type of engine oil is recommended for the Honda Activa110?

Arrow

<!-- image -->

Honda recommends 10W30 grade engine oil for the Activa110 series.

##### 7. What is the fuel tank capacity of the Honda Activa110?

Arrow

<!-- image -->

The Activa110 has a fuel tank capacity of 5.3 liters.

##### 8. Is the Honda Activa110 suitable for long-distance rides?

Arrow

<!-- image -->

Yes, with its comfortable design, fuel efficiency, and reliability, the Activa110 is ideal for daily commutes and moderate long-distance rides.

##### 9. What is the paid service schedule for Activa110?

Arrow

<!-- image -->

Every 6000 Kms or Within 6 months
