##### 1. What is the standard warranty period?

Arrow

Honda SP125 comes with a standard warranty of 3 years, 42000km (whichever is earlier) from date of purchase.

##### 2. What does standard warranty covers?

Arrow

Standard Warranty usually cover major components and system of the product This  also include repairs of the defect in component or workmanship.

##### 4. What is an Extended Warranty?

Arrow

An extended warranty is additional warranty coverage offered to customers in addition to the Standard Warranty. It can be purchased from the HMSI Authorized Dealers. You can opt Extend Warranty from 1 year up to 7 years - Total making of 10 years of warranty (3 + 7 years).

##### 6. Which Honda SP125 motorcycle parts are covered by the warranty?

Arrow

The warranty covers manufacturing and craftsmanship. There is no warranty coverage for parts like the clutch plate, brake shoe, tire and tube, chain and sprocket, etc., that wear out with frequent use.

##### 7. What is the free servicing schedule of the Honda SP125 motorcycle?

Arrow

During the free service period, there are no labour fees. You only need to pay for the replacement of consumables such as engine oil, grease, suspension oil, air cleaner elements, and fuel filters.

The following is the schedule for free services:

- **First Free Service:** 15-30 days or 750-1000 km
- **Second Free Service:** 165-180 days or 5500-6000 km
- **Third Free Service:** 350-365 days or 11,500-12,000 km
