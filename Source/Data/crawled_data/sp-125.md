## Frequently Asked Questions

##### 1. What is the standard warranty period?

Arrow

<!-- image -->

Honda SP125 comes with a standard warranty of 3 years, 42000km (whichever is earlier) from date of purchase.

##### 2. What does standard warranty covers?

Arrow

<!-- image -->

Standard Warranty usually cover major components and system of the product This  also include repairs of the defect in component or workmanship.

##### 3. What are common exclusion in warranty?

Arrow

<!-- image -->

HMSI does not warrant normal wear and tear items like Clutch Weight, Clutch Disc,

Brake shoe, Brake Pads, Brake Disc, Drive

belt, Drive Chain, Drive Chain Sprocket,

Wheel Rim (in case of misalignment and

bent), Bushes, Fasteners, Shims, Washers

and Electrical items like Bulbs, Fuses,

Rubber and Plastic Components like

Grommets, O-Rings, Bellows, Packings,

Gaskets, Oil Seals and Consumables like

Fuel Filter, Air Cleaner Element, Engine Oil,

Grease, Suspension Oil, and other items as

specified by HMSI.

##### 4. What is an Extended Warranty?

Arrow

<!-- image -->

An extended warranty is additional warranty coverage offered to customers in addition to the Standard Warranty. It can be purchased from the HMSI Authorized Dealers. You can opt Extend Warranty from 1 year up to 7 years - Total making of 10 years of warranty (3 + 7 years).

##### 5. What is the free service schedule for the Honda SP125?

Arrow

<!-- image -->

- 1st Free Service: 750-1000 km or 15-30 days
- 2nd Free Service: 5500-6000 km or 165-180 day
- 3rd Free Service: 11500-12000 km or 350-365 day

Days or Kilometers whichever comes earlier from the date of Sale

##### 6. Which Honda SP125 motorcycle parts are covered by the warranty?

Arrow

<!-- image -->

The warranty covers manufacturing and craftsmanship. There is no warranty coverage for parts like the clutch plate, brake shoe, tire and tube, chain and sprocket, etc., that wear out with frequent use.

##### 7. What is the free servicing schedule of the Honda SP125 motorcycle?

Arrow

<!-- image -->

During the free service period, there are no labour fees. You only need to pay for the replacement of consumables such as engine oil, grease, suspension oil, air cleaner elements, and fuel filters.

The following is the schedule for free services:

- **First Free Service:** 15-30 days or 750-1000 km
- **Second Free Service:** 165-180 days or 5500-6000 km
- **Third Free Service:** 350-365 days or 11,500-12,000 km

##### 8. What is the oil capacity of the SP125 Honda motorcycle?

Arrow

<!-- image -->

Oil Capacity - After draining: 0.85 litres (0.90 US qt, 0.75 Imp qt) Oil Capacity - After disassembly: 1.0 litres (1.1 US qt, 0.9 Imp qt)

##### 9. What grade of oil is appropriate for my  SP125 Honda motorcycle?

Arrow

<!-- image -->

SAE 5W-30 is the ideal oil grade for the motorcycle.

##### 10. What is the idle RPM of the SP125 Honda motorcycle?

Arrow

<!-- image -->

The idle RPM is 1500 ± 100 rpm.

##### 11. What is the standard warranty period of the Honda SP125 motorcycle?

Arrow

<!-- image -->

The standard warranty period for the motorcycle is 36 months or 42,000 km, whichever is earlier from the date of sale.

##### 12. What is the paid service schedule for SP125?

Arrow

<!-- image -->

Every 6000 Kms or Within 6 months
