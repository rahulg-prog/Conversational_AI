## Battery

Honda e: SWAP takes care of battery maintenance, so you don't have to.

### Swappable Battery

The Activa e: is powered by two 1.5 kwh Honda Mobile Power Pack e:s that can be easily swapped. The easy-to-hold handle and center of gravity allow for effortless carrying. No worries about charging the battery, just swap &amp; ride in style.

### Battery Swapping Station

Mobile Power Pack e:s can be swapped for fully charged units at battery swapping stations* around town, freeing you from waiting while the battery is recharged.

*Availability limited to certain countries and regions.
