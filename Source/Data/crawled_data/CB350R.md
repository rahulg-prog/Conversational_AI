## Frequently Asked Questions

##### 1. How does the HSTC feature enhance riding?

Arrow

<!-- image -->

Honda Selectable Torque Control prevents rear-wheel spin, ensuring stability on slippery surfaces and better control.

##### 2. Where can I buy spare parts for the CB350?

Arrow

<!-- image -->

Spare parts are available at any Honda BigWing Dealership across India, ensuring quality and compatibility. Be sure to purchase genuine parts to maintain the motorcycle's performance and reliability..

##### 3. Is financing available for the CB350?

Arrow

<!-- image -->

Yes, flexible EMI options and exchange programs are available through Honda's partner financial institutions. Explore these options and choose the plan that best fits your budget and financing needs.

##### 4. Can I use the CB350 for long-distance touring?

Arrow

<!-- image -->

Absolutely! The upright riding position, wide seat, refined engine, and 15-litre fuel tank make it perfect for long rides and weekend getaways.
