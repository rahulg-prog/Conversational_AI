## Frequently Asked Questions

##### What engine does the Honda CB650R have?

Arrow

The Honda CB650R is powered by a 649 cc, 4-stroke, 16-valve DOHC inline 4-cylinder engine with liquid cooling. It delivers 70 kW @12,000 rpm and 63 Nm of torque @9,500 rpm, offering smooth power delivery across all RPMs.

##### What technology features are included in the CB650R?

Arrow

The motorcycle features:

- Honda E-Clutch for seamless gear shifts
- 5-inch TFT display with Bluetooth connectivity via Honda RoadSync for navigation, calls, and music
- Honda Selectable Torque Control (HSTC) to adjust engine power via a handlebar switch
- SHOWA® SFF-BP front suspension for enhanced handling

##### What is the tank capacity of the CB650R?

Arrow

The 15.4-litre fuel tank provides ample range for long rides, while the PGM-FI fuel injection system ensures optimal fuel efficiency and throttle response.

##### How does the CB650R ensure rider comfort and safety?

Arrow

- Upright handlebar and 810 mm seat height for ergonomic riding
- Radial-mount front brakes (dual 310 mm discs) for superior stopping power
- LED headlight for improved visibility
- Twin-spar frame for stability and agility
