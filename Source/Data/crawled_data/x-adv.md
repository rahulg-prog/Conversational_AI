## Frequently Asked Questions

##### 1. What makes the Honda X-ADV unique among scooters?

Arrow

<!-- image -->

The Honda X-ADV combines the agility of a scooter with the rugged capability of an adventure bike, all in one.

##### 2. What kind of engine does the Honda X-ADV have?

Arrow

<!-- image -->

It's powered by a 745 cc parallel-twin engine delivering 43.1 kW and 69 Nm of torque.

##### 3. Is the Honda X-ADV suitable for long-distance touring?

Arrow

<!-- image -->

Yes. With a 13.2 L fuel tank, comfortable seat height, and upright riding stance, it's built for distance.

##### 4. How does the Dual Clutch Transmission benefit the ride?

Arrow

<!-- image -->

It provides smooth, seamless gear changes-ideal for both stop-and-go city traffic and open-road cruising.

##### 5. What technology is integrated into the Honda X-ADV?

Arrow

<!-- image -->

A 5-Inch TFT display with Honda RoadSync gives access to maps, music, and calls while on the go.
