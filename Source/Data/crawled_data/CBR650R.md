## Frequently Asked Questions

##### 1. What is the engine specification of the Honda CBR650R?

Arrow

<!-- image -->

The CBR650R is powered by a 649 cc, liquid-cooled, 4-stroke, 16-valve DOHC inline 4-cylinder engine. It delivers 70 kW   @12,000 rpm and 63 Nm of torque @9,500 rpm, with a compression ratio of 11.6:1. The refined intake system and PGM-FI fuel injection ensure smooth power delivery across all RPM ranges.

##### 2. What are the key technology features of the CBR650R?

Arrow

<!-- image -->

- Honda E-Clutch: Enables seamless gear shifts without manual clutch operation.
- 5-inch TFT display with Honda RoadSync: Offers Bluetooth connectivity for navigation, calls, and music.
- Honda Selectable Torque Control (HSTC): Adjustable traction control via a handlebar switch.
- SHOWA SFF USD front suspension: Enhances handling and cornering precision.

##### 3. What is the fuel tank capacity and mileage of the CBR650R

Arrow

<!-- image -->

The bike has a 15.4-litre fuel tank, ideal for long rides. Combined with the efficient PGM-FI fuel system, it balances performance and fuel economy, though exact mileage may vary based on riding conditions.

##### 4. How does the CBR650R ensure rider comfort and safety?

Arrow

<!-- image -->

- Sporting riding position: Aggressive yet comfortable for varied conditions.
- Radial-mount front brakes (dual 310 mm discs) and slipper clutch: Provide confident stopping power and reduce rear-wheel hop.
- LED headlight and twin-spar frame: Improve visibility and stability.
- 130 mm ground clearance: Tackles rough terrain with ease.

##### 5. What makes the CBR650R's design unique?

Arrow

<!-- image -->

Inspired by the CBR1000RR-R Fireblade, it features a track-inspired aggressive design, Y-spoke aluminium wheels for reduced weight, and a 4-into-1 exhaust system that delivers a signature inline-four growl while optimizing performance.
