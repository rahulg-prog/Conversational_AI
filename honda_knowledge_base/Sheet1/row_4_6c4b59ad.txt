The Honda SP125 is a bike equipped with a 4-stroke, SI engine boasting a displacement of 123.94cc, which complies with OBD2B standards. Delivering a maximum power output of approximately 8.24 kW at 7500 rpm, this model offers a balance of performance and efficiency. Its fuel system includes a fuel tank capacity of 11 liters, supporting an impressive mileage of 63 kmpl as reported online. The bike features front and rear tires sized 80/100-18 and 100/80-18 respectively, designed for stability and grip. Braking is managed through a combination of front disc or drum brakes and rear drum brakes, complemented by a CBS (Combined Braking System) with an equalizer for enhanced safety. Suspension duties are handled by a telescopic front setup and a hydraulic type rear suspension, contributing to comfortable ride dynamics. Technological enhancements on the SP125 include Honda’s eSP (enhanced Smart Power) system, PGM-FI (Programmed Fuel Injection), a fully digital meter, an idling stop system, and a USB-C port for convenient device charging. Safety features are robust, incorporating the CBS with equalizer and an engine cut-off mechanism. The bike’s core attributes highlight a stylish design paired with a digital console and alloy wheels, imparting both aesthetic appeal and functionality. Available in classic hues such as Decent Blue, Black, Red, and Green Metallic, the Honda SP125 maintains a seat height of 790mm, ensuring rider comfort across various conditions.
